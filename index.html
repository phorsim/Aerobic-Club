WHO WE ARE

Welcome to Aerobic Club!

Aerobic Club is a student community that encourages everyone to stay active, healthy and happy through music, dance and physical activities.

Our mission is to create a friendly environment where students can exercise, develop teamwork, improve confidence and make new friends.

GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need dance experience to join?

No experience is needed. Our sessions are welcoming to beginners, and you can follow along at your own pace.

What should I bring?

Wear comfortable clothes and sneakers, and bring a water bottle to stay hydrated.

How do I become a member?

Fill in the registration form below and choose the activity you are most interested in. Our team can follow up with session details.
